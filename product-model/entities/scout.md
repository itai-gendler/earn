# Scout

A talent Earn recommends to a sponsor for one of its listings, from the talent's skills and track record.

## Information kept

- **Match score** — how well the talent fits the listing
- **Dollars earned** — what the talent has earned on Earn
- **Matched skills** — the listing's skills the talent has
- **Invited** — whether the sponsor invited the talent to the listing
