# Account

The sign-in identity a person holds on Earn. One account serves the person as talent and as a member of any sponsor teams they join.

## Information kept

- **Email** — the email address the person signs in with
- **Email preferences** — the kinds of email the person receives: new listings, the weekly listing roundup, comments and likes on their work, scout invitations, replies and mentions, product news and newsletters, and for sponsors new submissions, comments on their listings and deadline reminders
- **Blocked** — whether the Earn team has blocked the person from taking part
